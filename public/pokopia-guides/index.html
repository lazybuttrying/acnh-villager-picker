Pokopia Guides

Every Pokemon in Pokemon Pokopia, organized by what decides your town: specialties, ideal habitats and types.

In Pokopia, a Pokemon's specialty is a working ability. Build builds houses, Chop fells trees, Water waters crops, and a town only runs when the right specialties live in it. Pokemon also move in where the habitat suits them, so the environment you build decides who arrives. These pages collect all 307 Pokemon by specialty, habitat and type, so you can plan before you build.
